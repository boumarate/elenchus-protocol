# Security Policy

## Status

Elenchus is pre-release and **unaudited**. Do not use it with real funds. Testnet only until a formal audit is complete.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue.

Use GitHub's private reporting: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. If that is unavailable, contact the maintainers at **<security-contact@your-domain>** (replace before publishing).

Include: affected component and commit, steps to reproduce, impact, and any proposed fix.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 5 business days.
- An assessment and a fix timeline after triage.
- Credit in the advisory if you want it.

## Scope

In scope: Soroban contracts in `contracts/`, the SDK, the indexer, and the web app. Out of scope: third-party services (Stellar network, IPFS gateways, wallets) and social engineering.

## Bounties

There is no bug bounty program yet.
