# Code of Conduct

Elenchus is about testing claims rigorously and respectfully. We hold ourselves to the same standard in how we treat each other.

## Our standard

- Critique ideas, code, and evidence. Never the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Be specific and constructive. Say what is wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here was new once.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when reviewing or deciding.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and misrepresenting research or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants who violate this code. Report concerns to the maintainers at **<conduct-contact@your-domain>** (replace before publishing). Reports are handled privately.
